# Security

Found a security problem in Riffscribe? Please email **admin@6x7.gr** with what you found and how to reproduce it. Do not open a public issue for it.

You will get a reply within a few days. Please give us reasonable time to fix the problem before you share it publicly.

What is in scope: riffscribe.6x7.gr, the accounts and saved-songs service behind it, and this repository. Audio and stems never leave the player's browser, so the interesting surface is sign-in, saved charts, billing and the assistant.
